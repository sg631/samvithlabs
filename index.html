The Beginning of a Career

I began programming when I was 6 years old with Scratch and Minecraft commands, which sparked my love for rationality and logic dating back to even my love of puzzles as a toddler.

2020

Webdev and Music

Around age 8 years old, I started learning to play the piano, and began my career in web development at this time, learning the fundamentals of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

2022

Singing and Robotics

At 10 years old, I started taking instruction for singing and started to involve myself more in music. I began to explore music composition, messing around with different DAWs until I settled upon LMMS with the idea that I would eventually switch to FL Studio. This was also the first year of doing First Lego League with a team.

2024

Guitar and CAC

At 12 years old, I started learning to play the guitar at the same place I was learning singing. I found it fun and easier than piano. This was the first year I participated in the annual Congressional App Challenge, started looking into React and development with modern frameworks, and was performing at many different events.

This was the first fully released project, initially created for the 2025 Congressional App Challenge. It was also the first project I made using React. Despite making numerous prototypes of various projects to different levels of completion, I had never actually finished one of my projects before this.
